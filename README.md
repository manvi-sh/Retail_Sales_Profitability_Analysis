# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

Excel-based business analytics project analyzing sales performance, profitability, regional/category trends, customer segments, products, and discount impact.

## Key Insights
- Total Sales: ₹22.97 Lakh
- Total Profit: ₹2.86 Lakh
- Profit Margin: 12.47%
- West leads regional sales performance.
- Technology is the top-performing category.
- Discount of 30%+ are associated with negative profit.

## Deliverables
- Excel analysis & interactive dashboard
- Executive presentation
- Business insights and recommendations
